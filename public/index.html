Navee’s Smart Scanner

Simulated feed
About the data. Quotes and search come from a free public market data source through this site’s own server, and are usually delayed by about 15 minutes. Search covers most listed NSE, BSE and global companies. If the source is unavailable or rate-limited, the app switches to a built-in sample feed and says so in the status. Market cap, P/E and some ratios are sample values for the pre-loaded companies and show “—” for others. The ribbon at the top lists sample corporate actions, not official SEBI, NSE or BSE announcements. Not investment advice.
